# Justification for Lightweight U-Net

A lightweight U-Net (such as MONAI's `BasicUNet` with reduced feature channels) was deliberately chosen for this task over a more complex or uncompressed architecture. For bounding box localization and coarse heart masking on non-contrast CT scans, the model does not need to learn exceedingly intricate, high-frequency textural details or sub-millimeter anatomical borders that a full TotalSegmentator pipeline targets. Instead, the task demands robust spatial awareness of large macro-structures, which a lightweight U-Net captures effectively while maintaining a target Dice score >0.85. The primary advantage of this approach is the massive speed improvement during inference: whereas the full TotalSegmentator pipeline sequentially processes dozens of classes and incurs a substantial computational bottleneck, this lightweight model focuses entirely on quickly regressing a single region of interest, significantly reducing parameters, memory footprint, and floating-point operations. This architectural efficiency directly translates to near real-time inference, making it vastly superior for deployment pipelines where immediate localization of the heart is required before applying downstream analytical workflows.
